Phytohormones - plants biologically active substances, they regulate growth, development and other phisiology actions

2 types:
- Stimulating
- Inhibutory

# Auxin
He is formed on root apex and on shoot apex. This <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">stimulating hormone</mark>. He increase root growth and shoot growth.

Auxin form on light and move to dark parts. He always move top down

## Mechanism
1) Auxin is moving to a cell. 
2) Move into cell(in a dark plant part)
3) Make cell membrane more softer
4) H20 move to the cell
5) Turgor increase
6) The cell stretches

# Gibberellin
He formed on plant buds, seeds, root apex, apical leaves. He too increase root and shoot <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">but causes seeds germination.</mark>

## Mechanism
1) Gibberellin activate under the h20 influence
2) Gibberellin through seed shield move to a endosperm. Substances stored in the form of a polymer. Participates on enzyme form. 
3) Enzyme convert polymers into monomers
4) Monomer move into a embryo

# Cytokinin
He form on roots. He too stimulate. He increase root and shoot growth, cause seeds germination and plant buds, <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">slows down leaf aging</mark>
## Slow down leaf aging mechanism
1) Increase protein, pigments synthesis
# Abscisic acid
Form on leafs, shoots, fruits, seeds. This is <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">inhibitory hormone</mark>. Stop plant growth, <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">stimulate closing the stomata to reduce transpiration</mark>, causes yellowing and leaf fall, cause stop seed germination. This reduce resource wasting
# Ethylene
He form <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">practically on every organ</mark>. He stop plant growth and stimulate fruits germination
