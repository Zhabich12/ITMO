# Ecological significance
In boreal forests bryophytes like **Pleurozium** form a shell for the nitrogen-fixing bacteria. They increase N2 availability. Other mosses inhabit extremal conditions like mountain peaks, tundra and deserts. Mosses can survive in hot conditions, drying up and regining moisture after rain. **Phenol components** in cell wall can **absorb ultraviolet radiation.**

Sphagnum mosses is a **main component of peat**. Sphagnum **doesn't decompose fast** partly the cell walls has phenol components. Peat bogs contain approximately **30% CO2 from world soil pool.** This tank help to **stabilize climate system**.
# Economical significance
Peat is a **fuel resource** for Europe and Asia. Today peat too use like fuel especially in Ireland and Canada. Peat mosses are used **as fertilizer and as package for transporting** because their cells can absorb water, the mass of which surpasses approximately 20 times greater than the mass of the moss itself.
