# Class monocots
## Order Liliales family Liliaceae
**Life forms** - herbs, rarely - shrubs, woods
**Vegetative organs** have rhizomes, bulbs
**Inflorescences** - brush
![[Pasted image 20260423010609.png]]
## Order Poales family Poaceae
**Life forms** - herbs
**Vegetative organs** - hollow stems, filled parenchyma, intercalary growth, the leaves are narrow and long
**Inflorescences** - complex colossus, panicle, cob
**Fruits** - grain

# Class dicotyledons
## Order Brassicales family Brassicaceae
**life forms** - annuals and perennial herbs, shrubs
**Vegetative organs** - simple leaves without stipules, biennials form roots 
**Inflorescences** - raceme
**Fruits** - pod
![[Pasted image 20260423011535.png]]
## Order Rosales family Rosaceae
**Life forms** - herbs, shrubs, trees
**Vegetative organs** - leaves with stipules
**Inflorescences** - raceme, corymb, umbel
**Fruits** - drupe, polynut, pome
![[Pasted image 20260423135506.png]]
## Order Solanales family nightshade
**Life forms** - herbs, shrubs, small trees
**Vegetative organs** - simple leaves without stipules, may have rhizomes and tubers
**Inflorescences** - cyme, solitary
**Fruits** - capsule, berry
![[Pasted image 20260423135513.png]]
## Order Fabales, family Legume
**Life forms** - herbs, shrubs, trees
**Vegetative organs** - compound leaves often with stipules
**Flowers** - Upper petal - standart(banner), two lateral petals - wings, two fused lower petals - keel
**Inflorescences** - raceme, head
**Fruits** - pod
![[Pasted image 20260423140306.png]]
## Order Asterales family Asteraceae
**Life forms** - herbs, shrubs, woods, vines
**Vegetative organs** - simple leaves often without stipules
**Flowers** - 4 species: 
1) Tubular - bisexual
2) Ligulate(ray)- bisexual
3) False-lingual - female or sterile
4) Funnel-shaped - sterile
**Inflorescences** - head
**Fruits** - achene
