# Muscle control
Muscles control by motor neurons and **motor units** - a collection of muscle cells. Motor neurons located inside the spinal cord in the gray matter. Their axons heading to the muscle, make contacts with every muscle fiber.

For smooth muscle contraction different motor units can contract asynchronously. Then one unit contract, other synthesis ATP for contract. 

**Reflex arc biology significance** - this allow increase contracts bypass the brain. This is actual when running on uneven surfaces case for example
