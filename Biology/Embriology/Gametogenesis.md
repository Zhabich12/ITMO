Gametogenesis - process then forming germ cells

There's male and female germ cells and gametogenesis have some stage for forming germ cells.

Gametogenesis:
- Spermatogenesis - then form male germ cells
- Oogenesis - then from female germ cells

Globally gametogenesis have 4 stages:
- Reproduction
- Growth
- Ripening
- Formation 

Laying of cells for gametogenesis happens on early ontogenesis. Because for reproduction need much cells primary germ cells can reproduct by mitosis. On spermatogenesis form billions germ cells, <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">on oogenesis forms only 1 egg by 1 cycle</mark>.
