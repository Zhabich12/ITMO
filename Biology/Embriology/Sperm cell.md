# Anatomy
Sperm cell consists of head, neck, mid piece and tail.
In head nucleus and acrosome. <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">The acrosome</mark> - modified golgi complex, he contains special enzymes dissolve a egg shell
In heck located 2 centrioles. From the 1 centriole starts axoneme. <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">Axoneme</mark> - axial thread with 9x2 + 2 structure.
In mid piece located axoneme, he surrounded by mitochondries. They make energy for moving.
# Properties
Has reotaxis - ability to move against flow
Has hemotaxis - ability to move to the substances allocated by a egg
